Honest notes

  • CopilotKit draws our atoms inside ITS wrapper and theme, so the custom-lane numbers measure whether the wrapper changes how our atoms look and behave, not CopilotKit's own components.
  • Status is derived from per-check results with rules version 2: any difference from React wins over a verification gap; the worst case wins for atoms with several cases.
  • Atoms with a pixel difference from React outside the allowance list, by environment: light 480 32, dark 480 32, light 360 32, dark 360 31. Overflow at 360 px: 10. Contrast worse than React: 14. Behaviour differs: 0.
  • 34 atoms are not verified because the example paints no visible text, 19 because they are blank on both sides in at least one environment and 17 because a check is unstable (time or animation dependent; they sit on the allowance list and record unstable, never pass).
  • 22 atoms have no example in the gate data and are not measured.
