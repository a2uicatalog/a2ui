Phone locked after too many PIN attempts

Issue

Your phone has locked itself after too many wrong PIN attempts, and shows a "phone locked" message.

Resolution

The phone unlocks itself after 30 minutes with no action needed. If you need access sooner:

  1. Use the reset link on the lock screen.
  2. Verify your identity using the method you set up (email code or authenticator app).
  3. Set a new PIN. It must be at least 6 digits and not one of your last 3 PINs.
  4. Unlock the phone with the new PIN.

If you do not have access to your recovery email, contact the support team for a manual identity check.
