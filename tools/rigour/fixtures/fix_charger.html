Laptop will not charge

Issue

The laptop battery does not charge, or the charging light stays off when the cable is plugged in.

Resolution

Work through these steps in order. After each step, plug the cable back in before moving to the next.

  • Check that the wall socket works by plugging in a lamp.
  • Restart the laptop completely (shut it down, not just close the lid).
  • Check that the charging cable is not damaged. A frayed cable is a common cause of slow or no charging.
  • Remove and reseat the charger plug. This clears a loose connection the laptop depends on.

If none of the above works, note the colour of the charging light and contact the support line with that detail -- it narrows the cause down quickly.
