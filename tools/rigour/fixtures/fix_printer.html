Inkjet printer prints faded pages or stays offline

Issue

A home inkjet printer prints faded pages, or shows as "offline" while jobs wait in the queue.

Resolution

SymptomLikely causeFix
Pages come out fadedThe ink cartridge is nearly emptyReplace the ink cartridge and print a test page.
Jobs wait in the queueA stuck job is blocking the queueOpen the print queue, cancel all pending jobs, then print one test page.
Printer shows offlineThe printer lost its Wi-Fi connectionPower-cycle the printer: turn it off, wait 30 seconds, turn it back on.

If the printer still shows offline after a power cycle, check that it is on the same network as your laptop.
