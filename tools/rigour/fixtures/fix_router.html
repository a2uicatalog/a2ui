Home router keeps dropping the connection

Issue

Your laptop joins the home Wi-Fi network but drops off every few minutes, or shows "limited connectivity".

Resolution

What happens when it drops?

  • It drops and reconnects on its own after a few seconds.
  • It drops and does not reconnect automatically -- you have to pick the network again.
  • It stays connected but shows "no internet" even though the signal is strong.

If it reconnects on its own: this is usually a weak signal. Move the router higher and away from thick walls, or check for another network with the same name nearby.

If it does not reconnect automatically: forget the network on your laptop and join it again, entering the password -- a damaged saved network profile is the usual cause.

If it shows "limited connectivity" with a strong signal: the laptop has a Wi-Fi link but no usable IP address. Restart the router (unplug it for ten seconds) and try again.
