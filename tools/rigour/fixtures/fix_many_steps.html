Reset a saved wireless network

Issue

Your laptop will not rejoin the home network after a password change.

Resolution

  1. Open the settings app.
  2. Select the network section.
  3. Choose the wireless tab.
  4. Find the saved networks list.
  5. Select the home network.
  6. Choose to forget it.
  7. Turn the wireless adapter off.
  8. Turn the wireless adapter back on.
  9. Join the home network again.
