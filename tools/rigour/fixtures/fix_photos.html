Photos not syncing to the laptop

Issue

New photos are not appearing on the laptop, even though they show up fine on the phone.

Resolution

Check sync settings first

  • Open the photo app's settings and confirm "Sync photos" (or similar) is turned on.
  • Confirm the sync interval is not set to "Manual" -- if it is, switch it to "Automatic".

If sync is already on

  • Sign out of the photo app on the phone and sign back in with the same account.
  • Confirm the phone has a working Wi-Fi or mobile data connection -- sync pauses entirely when there is no connection.

If signing out and back in does not fix it, the issue is most likely a full storage plan rather than the phone itself.
