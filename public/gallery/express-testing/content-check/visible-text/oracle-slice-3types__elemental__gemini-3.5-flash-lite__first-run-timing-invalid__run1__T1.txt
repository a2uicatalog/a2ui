 Never-Ending Journey
 Core Concepts
 STEP 1 
 Evaporation and Transpiration
 > Heat energy powers the first major phase of the water cycle. 
 STEP 2 
 Condensation
 > Cooling causes gas to turn back into liquid droplets. 
 STEP 3 
 Precipitation and Collection
 > Gravity pulls condensed moisture back to Earth's surface. 
 Worked Example
 REAL WORLD EXAMPLE 
 A Puddle on a Sunny Day
 Puddle Water (Liquid) + Solar Heat -> Water Vapour (Gas) -> Atmosphere The water did not vanish; it transformed states and joined the air.
 > Every puddle demonstrates evaporation right in front of our eyes.
