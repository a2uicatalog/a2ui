💧 The Water Cycle
 How water moves between oceans, air and land
 Earth Science 
 Three Core Concepts
 ☀️ 1. Evaporation
 The sun heats water in oceans, lakes and rivers. Liquid water turns into water vapor and rises into the atmosphere. Plants also release vapor through transpiration.
 ☁️ 2. Condensation
 As water vapor rises it cools and turns back into tiny liquid droplets around dust particles. Billions of droplets gather to form clouds.
 🌧️ 3. Precipitation and Collection
 When cloud droplets grow heavy enough, they fall as rain, snow, sleet or hail. The water runs off into rivers, soaks into the ground, or collects in oceans, and the cycle begins again.
 💡 Remember The water cycle has no start or end. The same water has been recycling for billions of years. 
 Worked Example
 WORKED EXAMPLE 
 Following one drop of seawater
 Ocean -> evaporation -> vapor -> condensation -> cloud -> precipitation (snow) -> melt and runoff -> river -> Ocean One possible path of a single water drop.
 > Water changes state, but it is never used up.
