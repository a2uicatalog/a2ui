Motion Timeline

A stage of child atoms choreographed on ONE clock, the way a product or launch video is: everything on screen is a pure function of time (seek(t)), so it plays, loops, scrubs, and can be frozen on any frame (open the page with #t=3.2). Children are any atoms, placed in percent of a fixed-size stage (so the composition is identical at every width) and addressed by id; tracks animate them by opacity, x/y, scale, rotate, 3D tilt, blur, wipe, and two custom properties the demo kit listens to (p = progress, step = which caption/nav item); a camera track pans, zooms and tilts the world while layer:"hud" children (captions) stay put. Each key is a DESTINATION: {t, x, ease} means arrive at x at time t using ease, like GSAP .to(); before a property's first key it holds that first value, so an element can be hidden until its first key. Ease is a token name from motion_tokens (expo-out, standard, quart-in-out, overshoot, hold...) or four cubic-bezier numbers; with bpm set, keys take beat instead of t so the piece sits on a grid. Style that reads as designed rather than generated: one orchestrated moment per scene, entrances 600 ms or less on expo-out, on-screen moves on standard, the camera on quart-in-out, a shared motion vector between scenes, restraint over noise (no particle bursts, neon glows or bouncy easing on UI). Honours prefers-reduced-motion (holds the poster frame; controls still work).

FieldType
titlestring (optional, max 80). Accessible name of the stage. Default "Motion sequence".optional
aspect"16:9" | "4:3" | "1:1" | "9:16" (optional, default "16:9"). The stage is laid out at 1280x720, 1200x900, 1000x1000 or 720x1280 logical px and scaled to the container.optional
durationnumber. Seconds, clamped 2-120. Default 12.optional
bpminteger. 0-240. When set, keys may use `beat` (t = beat * 60 / bpm) instead of `t`.optional
easestring | [x1,y1,x2,y2]. Default ease for keys that do not name one. A token from motion_tokens (not "hold"). Default "standard".optional
loopbool. Default true.optional
autoplaybool. Plays when scrolled into view, pauses when it leaves. Default true. Off, or under prefers-reduced-motion, it shows the poster frame.optional
controlsbool. Play/pause button, scrubber and clock. Default true.optional
posternumber. Seconds; the frame shown when not autoplaying. Default 60% of duration.optional
theme"dark" | "light" (optional, default "dark"). Stage background and control colours.optional
accent"#rrggbb". Glow and control accent. Default "#38bdf8".optional
background"#rrggbb". Stage background override.optional
backdrop"glow" | "grid" | "flat" (optional, default "glow")optional
blocksarray (max 24) of any atom blocks. Each may also carry id (^[a-z][a-z0-9_-]{0,31}$, makes it addressable by a track), place {x, y, w, h, z, origin} (x/y/w/h in percent of the stage; z 0-99; origin "c"|"tl"|"t"|"b"|"l"|"r" is the transform origin), and layer "world" (default, moves with the camera) or "hud" (stays on screen).required
tracksarray (max 40) of {target: id, keys: [...]} (max 48 keys each). A key is {t | beat, ease?, x?, y?, opacity?, scale?, rotate?, rx?, ry?, blur?, clip?, p?, step?}. x,y are the element's top-left in percent of the stage (for an element nested in another, an offset from its natural place); opacity 0-1; scale 0-6; rotate, rx, ry in degrees (tilt -80..80); blur 0-40 px; clip 0-1 is a left-to-right wipe; p 0-1 drives a demo atom's progress (chart draw, bar growth, toggles, fills, click ripple, orb level) and recounts its numbers; step is a float that crossfades captions and selects the active nav item. ease on a key is the ease of the segment ARRIVING at that key: a token name, "hold" (jump at the key), or four numbers.required
scenesarray (max 12) of {layer: id, t | beat, transition?}, optional. STITCHES scenes together: each layer (usually a motion_layer) is hidden until its start and hands over to the next scene instead of cutting. Within `overlap` seconds of the next scene's start the old one leaves while the new one arrives. transition is one of cut, dissolve, push, zoom-through, blur, rise, whip, wipe, iris, clock, slice, flip, spin, portal (with `portal: {x, y, w}`), ribbon and applies to the scene it names (the hand-over INTO it); without one the timeline's `stitch` is used. Your own tracks on the same layer are applied after these and win, so a scene can still do its own entrance.optional
morphsarray (max 6) of {from, to, t | beat, dur?, ease?}. Match cut: a copy of the `from` child flies from its box to the `to` child's box (resizing on the way) starting at t, over dur seconds (0.1-4, default 0.8), with ease (a motion token, default quart-in-out). from hides when the flight starts and to appears when it lands, so the morph owns their opacity: do not also give them opacity tracks. Both must have a numeric place.w and sit at the top level or directly inside a motion_layer; others are dropped and reported.optional
stitch"cut" | "dissolve" | "push" | "zoom-through" | "blur" | "rise" | "whip" | "wipe" | "iris" | "clock" | "slice" | "flip" | "spin" | "portal" | "ribbon" (optional, default "dissolve"). whip is a fast push with heavy motion blur; wipe reveals the new scene left to right over the old one; iris opens a circle from the centre; clock sweeps round like a clock hand; slice reveals through vertical bars; flip turns the old scene edge-on and the new one in; spin rotates and scales through; portal dives into a box in the old scene (give that scene entry `portal: {x, y, w}` in stage percent, the box keeps the stage shape) and the box becomes the new scene; without a box it falls back to dissolve; ribbon sweeps a slanted band in the accent colour across the frame and reveals the new scene behind it. The hand-over used by scenes that do not name one. Pick one per film: a shared motion vector between scenes is what reads as designed.optional
overlapnumber. Seconds a hand-over takes, 0-3. Default 0.6. Ignored by cut.optional
camera{keys: [{t | beat, ease?, x?, y?, zoom?, rx?, ry?, rz?, z?}]} (max 48 keys), optional. z (-1500 to 1500 px) dollies the camera through depth: top-level blocks with place.depth (0-1) sit further back and, with a compensating scale, look unchanged at z 0, so moving z gives real parallax. x,y are the focus point in percent of the stage (default 50,50); zoom 0.25-6 (default 1); rx, ry, rz tilt in degrees. Moves only layer:"world" children.optional
