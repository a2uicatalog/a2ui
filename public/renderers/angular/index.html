Honest notes

  • Measured against the React lane on the same code.
  • Status is derived from per-check results with rules version 4: any difference from React wins over a verification gap; the worst case wins for atoms with several cases.
  • Atoms with a pixel difference from React, by environment: light 480 3, dark 480 3, light 360 3, dark 360 3. Overflow at 360 px: 0. Contrast worse than React: 0. Behaviour differs: 0.
  • 29 atoms are not verified because the example paints no visible text, 20 because they are blank on both sides in at least one environment and 0 because a check is unstable (time or animation dependent; non-deterministic measurements only: they sit on the allowance list and record unstable, never pass).
  • 22 atoms have no example in the gate data and are not measured.
  • 3 atoms fail the pixel rule in all four environments (light and dark, 480 and 360 px).
  • Deterministic mode: every shot was taken twice on the shipped build (frozen Date, virtual clock, seeded random, fixed raster settings). 15 of 20 renderer-and-environment cells are byte-identical; the other 5 differ in one atom by at most 15 pixels of dither (channel-sum delta at most 2 of 765) and pass the pixel rule. So pixel results are plain pass or fail with no allowance list. The custom-text check is also deterministic (two identical passes). This holds only for a fixed id order and 12-case chunking (chunk 1 against chunk 12 differs for 10 of 33 test atoms), on the slicer image that took the baselines; the old local custom runner is not deterministic. Painting and visible text (and behaviour where it was run) still come from one earlier, non-deterministic run.
  • Seeded-random atoms: gmail_unread_count, match_exercise are drawn with a note: their random draws are consumed per renderer, so parity with React is excluded for them. Open item: reseed per case in the slicer.
