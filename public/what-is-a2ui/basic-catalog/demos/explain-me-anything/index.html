Good to know

Limits

Drawings have at most 12 boxes, 16 arrows and 4 groups. Our code checks each drawing against these limits before it shows it.

For developers: both catalog routes and details

A macro pack, in our catalogue, is a bundle of small pieces of code that turn a few lines of text into finished pictures. This one turns Mermaid into an animated SVG. "Macro pack" is our term; A2UI itself calls the building blocks components.

How the live demo works and where your data goes

Try it yourself

Type a question. Your own Gemini key asks the model from this tab, the model writes a short diagram as text, our drawing service draws it, and you get the picture and the steps.

  1. Your question
  2. Your key calls the model
  3. Our Worker draws
  4. Picture and steps

Where your data goes

  • Your question goes to Google (the Gemini API), under Google's terms, using your key. We never receive it.
  • Free-tier prompts may be read by Google's reviewers. As of 16 November 2026, Google's Gemini API terms say that on the free tier "human reviewers may read, annotate, and process your API input and output", and that Google uses the content to improve its products. Paid use is treated differently. Check Google's current terms before you rely on any of this.
  • The diagram text the model writes (not your key, not your question) goes to our drawing service. It draws the picture and keeps no copy. It writes one log line per request with the route, the result code, the size of the request and whether it was refused, and it keeps a count of drawings per day. It does not log the text, any key or the check token, and our code does not log your address. To limit abuse it counts requests under a one-way hash of your address and the date.
  • Your key stays in this tab. It is held in memory, sent only to Google, never put in the address and never sent to us. A browser extension or a script on this page could read it, so use a throwaway key and delete it afterwards.
